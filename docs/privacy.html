Privacy policy

Tring (package io.github.jaswantk917.chitthighanti) · Effective 8 November 2026

In short: everything the app reads stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers of ours. Nothing you send, receive or call is collected or shared.

What the app reads, and why

  • SMS and MMS, only while the app is your default SMS app: to show, sort, send, schedule and notify you about messages, and, on the phone side, to spot "out for delivery" messages so a delivery rider's call can ring.
  • Call log and phone state, only while the app is your default phone app: to show your recent calls and to handle calls.
  • Contacts: to show names and photos, and to tell saved people from strangers.
  • Notifications from other apps, only if you turn on notification access: to notice when an order or ride is on its way (for example a food or courier app saying "on the way"). The text is checked on the phone, and only a short note is kept: the company, whether it's on its way, and any phone number in the message, for up to a day.
  • Android's block list, only while the app is your default SMS or phone app: to block and unblock numbers you choose.

What stays on your phone

Messages, calls, contacts, call notes, the labels you give callers, your blocking rules and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device or in Android's own databases. Sorting and suggested replies run on the device: with built-in rules and, on phones that support it, Google's on-device Gemini Nano model, which does not send your messages anywhere.

What touches the internet

  • MMS pictures are downloaded from and sent through your mobile carrier, as with any SMS app.
  • Business logos: for messages from known companies, the app asks Google's public favicon service for that company's logo by its website address (for example amazon.in). No message content, phone number or identifier is sent.

The app has no other network traffic. It contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries.

Sharing

No personal or sensitive data is collected, sold or shared with anyone.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, removes everything it stored. Your SMS and call history stay in Android's own databases, as they would with any other SMS or phone app. Numbers you blocked stay on Android's block list until you unblock them.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/jaswantk917/chitthi-ghanti.
